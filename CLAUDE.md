# Jerusalem-AI — agent notes

A public, hand-curated index of AI in Jerusalem. The whole index is `README.md`;
inclusion rules are in `SCOPE.md`. Read SCOPE.md before adding anything.

## Relationships

- **Parent:** [Israeli-AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI)
  (local clone `~/repos/indexing-repos/Israeli-AI`) links here from its
  Related Indexes table. Jerusalem entries that are already in Israeli-AI
  (DataTalks JLM, the AI JLM Facebook group) are repeated here on purpose — this
  index is the city view.
- **Sibling:** [Jerusalem-Projects-Index](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Jerusalem-Projects-Index)
  holds non-AI Jerusalem projects and cross-references this repo.
- **Predecessor:** [Jerusalem-Tech-Ecosystem](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Jerusalem-Tech-Ecosystem)
  is an older, broader tech-ecosystem list. Not merged; linked from Related.

## Conventions

- Tables: `Name | Description`, plus a `Stars` column with a shields.io badge for
  GitHub repos. Descriptions are neutral and factual — no marketing copy.
- Only list public repos and public resources.
- People: public professional role only, linked to an institutional or official
  profile. Never list private individuals.
- Companies HQ'd elsewhere with a Jerusalem R&D site go in their own table,
  labelled as such.
- Bump the `Last updated` date (top and bottom of README) on every content change.
- Seeded 2026-10-15. External entries were web-verified on that date; re-check
  links before relying on an entry older than a few months.
