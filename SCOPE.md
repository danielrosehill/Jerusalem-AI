# Scope

This index tracks **AI in the city of Jerusalem** — the projects, organisations,
communities and public figures that make up the local AI ecosystem.

## The test

An entry must be **both**:

1. **AI-related** — machine learning, LLMs, agents, computer vision, NLP, AI
   research, or an organisation / community whose focus is AI; **and**
2. **Jerusalem-specific** — based or operating in Jerusalem, or (for projects
   and datasets) about Jerusalem as a subject.

## In scope

- Research labs, centres and departments in Jerusalem institutions working on AI
- Companies and startups headquartered in Jerusalem with AI as a core product.
  Major Jerusalem R&D sites of companies headquartered elsewhere are listed
  separately and labelled as such
- Accelerators, investors and ecosystem bodies based in Jerusalem with a
  meaningful AI focus
- Communities, meetups, hackathons and events held in Jerusalem
- Public figures whose public role is AI work in Jerusalem — founders, lab
  heads, faculty. Listed by role, linked to an official or public profile
- Municipal and government AI initiatives in Jerusalem
- Open-source AI projects about Jerusalem: agents, LLM tooling, AI-generated
  work with its method published
- **Datasets about Jerusalem** that are usable for training, agent grounding or
  analysis — labelled detection sets, image sets, sensor time series, geodata
  such as shelter locations. This differs from the parent
  [Israeli-AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI) index, which sends
  datasets to Israel-Reference-Repos; at city scale the datasets are few enough,
  and close enough to the projects that use them, to keep together here

## Out of scope

- Israel-wide AI work with no Jerusalem connection — that belongs in
  [Israeli-AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI)
- Jerusalem projects that are neither AI nor data — civic surveys, home
  automation, specific addresses. Those belong in
  [Jerusalem-Projects-Index](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Jerusalem-Projects-Index)
- Companies that merely have a sales or satellite office in Jerusalem
- Private individuals. People are listed only for a public, professional role

When in doubt: *is this both AI and Jerusalem?* Both must be true — except for
Jerusalem datasets, which qualify on being usable as AI input.
