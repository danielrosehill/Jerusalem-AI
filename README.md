# Jerusalem AI

A map of artificial intelligence in Jerusalem, Israel: research labs, companies,
ecosystem bodies, communities, events, public figures, municipal initiatives, and
open-source AI projects and datasets about the city.

The city view of the [Israeli AI Ecosystem](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI)
index. See [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md) for what gets in — the test is *both* AI *and*
Jerusalem.

## Hebrew University of Jerusalem

| Name | Description |
|---|---|
| [Benin School of Computer Science and Engineering](https://www.cs.huji.ac.il/) | Hebrew University's CS school; home of most of its ML, NLP and vision faculty |
| [Center of Knowledge in Machine Learning and AI](https://mlai.cs.huji.ac.il/) | University ML/AI centre — learning theory, vision, NLP, computational neuroscience |
| [Center for Interdisciplinary Data Science Research (CIDR)](https://cidr.huji.ac.il/) | University-wide data science research and training initiative |
| [Edmond and Lily Safra Center for Brain Sciences (ELSC)](https://elsc.huji.ac.il/) | Cognitive, biological and computational neuroscience |
| [Federmann Center for the Study of Rationality](https://ratio.huji.ac.il/) | Interdisciplinary centre; current research clusters include tech accountability |
| [Federmann Cyber Security Research Center – Cyber Law Program](https://csrcl.huji.ac.il/) | Joint law and CS programme; publishes on AI governance and AI in administrative law |
| [Jerusalem Center for Urban Innovation](https://urbaninnovation.huji.ac.il/) | Urban research labs and a smart-cities programme, with links to the municipality |
| [HUJI NLP Lab](https://github.com/huji-nlp) | NLP lab (Omri Abend) — GitHub organisation |
| [SLAB-NLP](https://github.com/SLAB-NLP) | Computational linguistics lab (Gabriel Stanovsky) — GitHub organisation |
| [Schwartz Lab](https://schwartz-lab-huji.github.io/) | NLP lab (Roy Schwartz) |

# Datasets and Models About Jerusalem

Published for machine learning.

| Resource | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [jerusalem-poster-detection](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/jerusalem-poster-detection) | Dataset | Geotagged street photos of a postering campaign in central Jerusalem, with per-design bounding boxes |
| [jerusalem-poster-detector](https://huggingface.co/danielrosehill/jerusalem-poster-detector) | Model | YOLO11n fine-tune trained on the dataset above — proof of concept for flyposting surveys |
| [Jerusalem-Streetscapes](https://huggingface.co/datasets/danielrosehill/Jerusalem-Streetscapes) | Dataset | 120 photos of Jerusalem's changing urban landscape, day and night (2024–2025) |
